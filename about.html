About

3def is a 3D design and visualization studio. We turn files, sketches and ideas into images people believe.

We work with product companies, architects and agencies who need to show something before it is built, or show it better than a camera can.

Every project gets the same care whether it is a single packshot or a full launch campaign.

How we work

Accuracy first

Proportions, materials and light are checked against your references, so the render matches what ships or gets built.

Decisions early

We agree composition and camera before spending time on detail. Changes are cheap at the start and expensive at the end.

Assets you can reuse

Models are built clean, so the same files can power next season's colorways, an animation or a web viewer.
