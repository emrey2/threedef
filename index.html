How a project runs

Clear steps, few surprises.

  1. Brief

    We agree on the goal, the shots, the deadline and the price before any modeling starts.

  2. Block out

    Rough models and camera angles, so you approve the composition early and cheaply.

  3. Refine

    Materials, lighting and detail. Feedback rounds are built into the schedule.

  4. Deliver

    Final files in the formats and sizes you need, ready for print, web or broadcast.
