Services

Four ways we help teams show what they are making.

Product visualization

Renders built from your CAD files or from sketches. Every material, color and finish can change without a new photo shoot, and the same model works for packaging, ecommerce and ads.

  • Packshots
  • Lifestyle scenes
  • Colorways
  • Exploded views
  • Packaging

Architectural visualization

Interiors, exteriors and aerial views from drawings or BIM models. Made for planning applications, competitions, off-plan sales and investor decks.

  • Exteriors
  • Interiors
  • Aerials
  • Masterplans
  • 360 panoramas

Animation & motion

Product films, explainers and short loops. We plan the story and camera moves with you, then deliver cuts sized for each channel.

  • Product films

  • Social loops
  • Fly-throughs
  • Title sequences

Interactive 3D

Real-time scenes that run in the browser: configurators, product viewers and AR previews. Optimized to load fast on phones.

  • Configurators

  • Unreal & Unity
  • Optimized assets

Questions we get asked

What do you need from us to start?

Whatever exists: CAD or BIM files, drawings, sketches, reference images, brand guidelines. If you only have an idea, we can model from scratch.

How is a project priced?

Per project, based on the number of images or seconds of animation, the level of detail and the deadline. You get a fixed quote after the brief.

How many rounds of feedback are included?

Feedback rounds are agreed in the quote and built into the schedule, with checkpoints at block-out and before final render.

Which file formats do you deliver?

High resolution stills (TIFF, PNG, JPG), video in the codecs and aspect ratios you need, and optimized 3D assets (glTF, USDZ, FBX) for real-time work.

Can you work with our agency or in-house team?

Yes. We often work as the 3D partner behind an agency or alongside an internal design team.

Have a product, space or idea to show?

Send a short brief. We will reply with questions, an approach and a quote.
